Privacy Policy

Focus · Last updated July 27, 2026

Focus is a personal productivity and mindfulness app. This policy explains what the app collects, what it does not, and the choices you have.

The short version: the only information Focus collects is the email address you use to sign in. Everything you write in the app — your reflections, journal entries, planner items, task lists, profile name and photo — is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your email address and a securely hashed version of your password on Google's servers. We never see or store your password in a readable form.

As part of providing sign-in, Firebase Authentication may also process technical information such as your IP address and basic device information for security purposes (for example, detecting abuse). This is described in Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's privacy documentation.

Nothing else leaves your device. In particular:

DataWhere it lives
Reflections and journal entriesOnly on your device
Planner items and task listsOnly on your device
Profile name and photoOnly on your device
Timer and breathing activityOnly on your device
Appearance and other settingsOnly on your device

Information we do not collect

Focus does not:

  • Collect analytics or usage statistics (Firebase Analytics is not included in the app, and analytics is disabled in its configuration)
  • Include advertising, ad networks, or tracking of any kind
  • Access the advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across apps or websites
  • Sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties
  • Use your content to train machine-learning models
  • Require or request access to your contacts, location, microphone, or health data

Photos

If you choose to add a profile photo, the image you pick is copied into the app's private storage on your device. Focus only receives the single image you select — it does not read your photo library. Your photo is never uploaded.

Widgets

If you add a Focus widget to your Home Screen, the app saves a small copy of your current day's task titles into a private, app-specific shared container on your device so the widget can display them. This data never leaves your device and is erased when you sign out or delete your account.

How your information is used

Your email address is used solely to:

  • Create and secure your account
  • Sign you in
  • Let you recover access to your account (password reset)

We do not send marketing email.

Data retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the app: Profile → Delete Account.

Deleting your account immediately and permanently:

  • Deletes your account and email address from Firebase Authentication
  • Deletes all app data stored on your device for that account, including your reflections, journal entries, planner items, task lists, profile name and photo

This action cannot be undone, and we retain no backup copy of your content.

Deleting the app from your device also removes all locally stored data, though your account record will remain until you delete the account.

Security

Passwords are handled and stored by Firebase Authentication using industry standard hashing; we never have access to them. Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security and encryption.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Focus is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with their email address, please contact us and we will delete the account.

International users

Firebase Authentication is operated by Google and your email address may be processed and stored on servers located in the United States or other countries.

Your rights

Because the only personal information we hold is your email address, you can exercise your rights simply:

  • Access / portability: your content is already on your device and under your control
  • Correction: update your profile in the app
  • Erasure: delete your account in the app (see Data retention and deletion)

If you are in the EEA or UK, our legal basis for processing your email address is the performance of our contract with you (providing an account).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
